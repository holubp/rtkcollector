An unresolved `normative` conflict blocks affected drafting, implementation and
compliance. A `non-normative` question may remain pending and visible only when
it does not change the affected contract or assurance scope.

An agent may fill analysis and recommendation fields. It MUST leave the approval
identity, timestamp and approved decision pending until a human explicitly
decides.
